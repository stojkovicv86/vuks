Sound familiar?

Six ways a site quietly loses visibility

New content ships every day, and traffic is still sitting at the crossroads.

travel_explore

Crawl budget is spent on junk

Google crawls filters, parameters and pagination while new pages wait weeks to be indexed. Log files show it immediately, Search Console only later.

language

The hreflang network is incomplete

One-way links, wrong region codes and pages missing from the set. The result: the wrong version of the site ranks in the wrong market.

link

Canonicals say one thing, internal links another

When signals disagree, Google decides for you. It usually picks the page you never wanted ranking.

alt_route

Redirect chains after every migration

Three hops to the final URL, lost link equity and pages nobody crawls anymore. The migration is over, the consequences are not.

rocket_launch

A deploy breaks SEO and nobody notices

A noindex survives from staging, robots.txt gets overwritten, schema disappears. Without QA before and after the deploy, it surfaces a month later.

confirmation_number

Recommendations sit in a ticket

An 80-page audit the dev team cannot prioritise. Without translating it into concrete tasks and verifying on staging, nothing gets implemented.
