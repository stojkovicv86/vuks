Three ways a site quietly loses visibility

New content ships every day, and traffic is still sitting at the crossroads. The reasons pile up: an incomplete hreflang network that sends the wrong version of the site to the wrong market, redirect chains left over from a migration, slow rendering that serves the bot an empty page, duplicates on parameter URLs, recommendations that sit in a ticket for months.
