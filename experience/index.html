Three engagements, three different technical problems. What the situation was, what I owned and what the hardest part of the job turned out to be.

Technical SEO across a portfolio of 20+ sites

Nexplay Media SA
Technical SEO Executive · 2026—present

The situation. Twenty-plus WordPress sites in the crypto and iGaming verticals, each with its own stack, publishing pace and migration history. No single audit covers them all — what is needed is a repeatable system.

What I own. Technical audits through Screaming Frog, JetOctopus and Lumar; log analysis to understand crawl behaviour and optimise crawl budget; implementing and validating hreflang networks; resolving redirect chains, broken links and canonical conflicts.

The hardest part. Preventing SEO regression on a portfolio that never stops changing — QA before and after deploys, staging verification and validation of SEO-critical elements during migrations, all through the dev teams’ ticketing systems.

International SEO across multiple markets

Clickout Media
Technical SEO Executive · 2024—2026

The situation. Several WordPress sites targeting different markets and languages. Incomplete hreflang networks, redirects inherited from old structures, and indexation that was unpredictable from site to site.

What I owned. Implementing technical SEO tasks across the portfolio, working on hreflang structures and international SEO, and systematically resolving redirect, broken-link and indexation issues.

The hardest part. A task-driven environment where a recommendation counts only once implemented. Working through ticketing systems and spreadsheets, aligning SEO and dev teams on priorities that shifted weekly.

Junior Technical SEO, freelance · 2022—2024

The situation. Other people’s audits, clear specifications and WordPress sites where a change has to be right the first time. There was no room for improvisation.

What I did. Executing technical SEO tasks to specification, optimising the WordPress backend — canonicals, redirects, hreflang — and implementing changes based on SEO audits.
