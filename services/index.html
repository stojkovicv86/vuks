Service 01

Technical SEO audit

The problem: the existing audit is a list of eighty errors in no particular order. The dev team does not know what comes first, so nothing gets done — or the easiest thing does, which changes nothing.

What I do: a full-site crawl through Screaming Frog, JetOctopus or Lumar, a review of Search Console data and recent changes, then findings ranked on an impact × effort matrix with an explanation of why something waits.

What you get
checkFindings prioritised by impact, not by error count
checkA task list the dev team can execute directly
checkA definition of done for every task
checkA walkthrough call with the team, not a PDF that means nothing to anyone
Service 02

Crawl budget and log analysis

The problem: new pages wait weeks to be indexed while Googlebot crawls filters, parameters and pagination. Search Console shows it a month later; the logs show it immediately.

What I do: server log analysis to see what Googlebot actually crawls and how often, then I close the leaks — robots rules, parameters, internal linking and a sitemap that matches the real state of the site.

What you get
checkA clear picture of where crawl budget goes
checkConcrete rules for what should not be crawled
checkFaster indexation of new content
checkSitemap and internal links aligned with priorities
Service 03

Indexation and site architecture

The problem: canonicals say one thing, internal links another and the sitemap a third. When signals disagree, Google decides for you — usually on the page you never wanted ranking.

What I do: a review of canonical logic, clean-up of redirect chains, repair of the internal link structure and control over what is allowed into the index at all — so every signal says the same thing.

What you get
checkCanonicals, sitemap and internal signals in agreement
checkRedirect chains reduced to a single hop
checkAn index holding only the pages that belong there
checkA link structure that passes strength where it is needed
Service 04

Hreflang and international SEO

The problem: one-way links, wrong region codes and pages missing from the set. The result is the wrong language version ranking in the wrong market.

What I do: setting up and validating complete hreflang networks across languages and markets, making sure every version has its pair and that the sets do not fall apart at the next publish.

What you get
checkA validated hreflang network with no one-way links
checkThe right language version in the right market
checkLess cannibalisation between localised pages
checkA process your team can repeat for a new market
Service 05

SEO QA and migrations

The problem: a noindex survives from staging, robots.txt gets overwritten, schema disappears after a redesign. Without checks before and after the deploy, it surfaces a month later — through a traffic drop.

What I do: a QA cycle around every deploy, staging verification and validation of SEO-critical elements during a migration — redirect mapping, pre-launch checks and monitoring right after.

What you get
checkA check-list for before and after every deploy
checkA migration without lost URLs or link equity
checkEarly detection of regressions, while they are still fixable
checkA dev team that knows what to check without me
Service 06

A new site on the right foundation

The problem: the site looks good but has no SEO logic — improvised structure, no measurement set up, and the first audit arrives a year later asking for everything to be rebuilt.

What I do: I design and build the site — from a one-pager up to fifteen pages — with structure and a keyword plan before the build, technical SEO built in along the way and GSC/GA4 measurement from day one. I do not build shops.

What you get
checkSite structure and a keyword plan before the first page
checkA site of up to 15 pages, ready to be indexed
checkGSC and GA4 measurement in place
checkA guide for running it yourself after handover
