Before you ask

The questions that come up most on intro calls

Do you work with small sites?

Yes, but honestly: below about fifty pages technical SEO rarely carries most of the result. I will say so on the call, before you spend the budget.

What do you need from us?

Access to Search Console and analytics, server logs if they exist, and one person from the dev team as a contact. Without those, the work is guesswork.

Do you implement the changes yourself?

On WordPress sites yes, within an agreed scope. On larger systems I write tickets and verify the implementation on staging.

When do results show?

Indexation and crawl respond in weeks, rankings in months. Anyone promising faster is selling something else.

Do you build shops?

Technical SEO for existing eCommerce sites, yes. Building a shop, no. For that you need a developer, not me.

How is it billed?

Audits: half upfront, half on delivery. Monthly engagements are invoiced in advance, with one month notice.
